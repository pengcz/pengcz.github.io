Dr. Changzhi Peng

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Multiscale Dynamics group at CWI in Amsterdam, working on electrical breakdown in gases and multiscale plasma modelling.

My research connects experiments and computation across scales — from streamers and leaders to metre-scale sparks and lightning — with applications in high-voltage technology and environmentally friendly insulating gases.
